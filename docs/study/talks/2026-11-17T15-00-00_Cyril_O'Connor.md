## 主讲人简介
Cyril O 'Connor is an internationally renowned scholar in mineral processing, a Founding Fellow of the Academy of Science of South Africa, a Fellow of the South African Academy of Engineering, and Emeritus Professor at the University of Cape Town. A former President of the International Mineral Processing Council, he has received the field's top honours, including the Antoine M. Gaudin Award and the Plaksin Medal. He was awarded the 2026 Chinese Government Friendship Award on 30 September 2026.

## 讲座简介
The interaction of water with solid surfaces underpins many physicochemical processes, notably mineral flotation, which depends on a particle being sufficiently hydrophobic to attach to a bubble. This paper reviews the fundamentals defining mineral hydrophobicity—surface energy, surface tension, roughness, interfacial energy and the work of adhesion of water—together with the origins of natural hydrophobicity and the means by which collectors impart it. Methods for measuring and quantifying hydrophobicity are surveyed, including contact angle, heat of immersion and excess Gibbs energy, and the mechanisms proposed for hydrophobic particle–bubble attachment are briefly addressed.
