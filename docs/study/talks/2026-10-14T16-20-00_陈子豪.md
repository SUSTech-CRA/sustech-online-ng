## 讲座简介
Liii STEM 面向数理学科学习场景，将专业的公式排版、结构化笔记与AI学习辅助相结合，帮助学习者更高效地记录、整理和理解复杂知识。本讲通过实际操作介绍如何利用 Liii STEM 完成数理笔记记录，并结合AI 提升学习与知识整理效率。Liii STEM is designed for STEM learning scenarios, combining professional formula typesetting, structured note-taking, and AI-powered learning assistance to help record, organize, and understand complex knowledge more efficiently. This workshop introduces how to use Liii STEM to take notes through hands-on practice, and to integrate AI tools to enhance the efficiency of learning and knowledge organisation.
