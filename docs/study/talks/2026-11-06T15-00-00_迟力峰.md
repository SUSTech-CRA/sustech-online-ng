## 讲座简介
On-surface chemistry enables the synthesis of functional molecules and polymers that are difficult to obtain through conventional synthetic methods by facilitating the activation, diffusion, and coupling of precursor molecules on surfaces. A key advantage of this approach lies in its precise control over product structures, which can be achieved through various strategies, including rational precursor design, interactions with substrate lattices, molecular preassembly, and single-molecule manipulation. Representative products reported to date include structurally tunable graphene nanoribbons, graphdiyne nanowires, conjugated polyenes, organic spin-bearing molecules, and metal-organic hybrids. Advanced experimental techniques, such as scanning tunneling microscopy (STM), noncontact atomic force microscopy (nc-AFM), scanning tunneling spectroscopy (STS), and single-molecule manipulation, enable precise characterization of the on-surface reactions and fundamental physical properties of these carbon-based functional molecules and polymers. The development of on-surface chemistry provides new concepts and approaches for the atomically precise synthesis and characterization of emerging molecular materials.
