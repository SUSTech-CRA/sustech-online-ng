## 主讲人简介
Prof. Katsuhiko Tomooka received his Ph.D. from Keio University in 1988. From 1988 to 2006, he was an assistant and associate professor at the Tokyo Institute of Technology. From 1991 to 1993, he was a postdoctoral fellow at Stanford University. Since 2007, he has been a professor at Kyushu University. His research interests include developing synthetic methodologies and performing the asymmetric synthesis of natural and non-natural chiral molecules. Awards (selected): 2010: SSOCJ Astellas Award for Organic Chemistry in Life Science; 2010: The CSJ Award for Creative Work; 2019: Synthetic Organic Chemistry Award, Japan; 2021: Molecular Chirality Award; 2022: Yagami Award; 2024: Award of Society of Silicon Chemistry.

## 讲座简介
To obtain enantioenriched chiral organic molecules, numerous optical resolution and asymmetric synthetic methods have been developed. Recently, we have developed new approach called dynamic asymmetric induction (DYASIN) for the preparation of enantioenriched chiral molecules based on the stereocontrol of dynamic chiral molecules (DYCMs) using an outer chiral source (OCS).
