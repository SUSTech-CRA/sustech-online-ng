## 讲座简介
The Harmony ecosystem is developing rapidly, with the number of applications and services within the ecosystem exceeding 400,000, and the number of registered developers reaching over 11 million. User experience and the maturity of the ecosystem continue to improve.
To continuously empower developers, the Harmony Ecosystem (Shenzhen) Innovation Center has launched the "Harmony Ecosystem · Star Academy" talent cultivation program. The courses provide systematic teaching and are delivered by expert lecturers, helping developers master core technologies such as application development, multi-platform deployment, and distributed development. After completing the training and passing the assessment, participants can obtain the official Huawei certification for HarmonyOS professionals. Outstanding participants also have the opportunity to be promoted to certified lecturers, participate in textbook compilation, and teach on the platform, providing comprehensive support for the upgrade of Harmony and ecosystem innovation.
The program is currently recruiting participants at Southern University of Science and Technology. The first 100 developers who pass the review can participate in the training for free.
