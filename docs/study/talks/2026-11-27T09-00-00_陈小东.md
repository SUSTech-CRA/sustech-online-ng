## 主讲人简介

## 讲座简介
Life communicates through ions, while machines operate through electrons. Bioelectronics bridges these languages, enabling technologies that sense biological activity, stimulate physiological processes, and deliver adaptive treatments. This talk will explore the materials chemistry underlying bio-integrated electronics, emphasizing soft interfaces that integrate intimately with living systems while remaining functional under deformation. Examples from our group, including supramolecular materials, skin-like interfaces that digitize muscle activity, and plant interfaces that monitor and modulate electrophysiological signals, will demonstrate emerging opportunities in human–machine interaction. The talk will also highlight the interdisciplinary challenges that must be overcome to translate flexible bioelectronics into reliable, scalable, and clinically meaningful technologies.
