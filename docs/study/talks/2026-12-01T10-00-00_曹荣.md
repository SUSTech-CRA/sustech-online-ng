## 讲座简介
Porous frameworks include metal–organic frameworks (MOFs), covalent organic frameworks (COFs), and hydrogen-bonded organic frameworks (HOFs). They feature highly ordered active sites, well-defined chemical and pore structures, and strong adsorption and enrichment capacity, showing broad application prospects in catalysis. In view of this, we have developed strategies for catalytic site design, structural regulation, cooperative catalysis and tandem catalysis. By utilizing framework nodes and ligands, intrinsic active sites on frameworks, and introducing active sites, we efficiently realize catalytic carbon dioxide conversion, water splitting, synthesis of hydrogen peroxide, and organic synthesis reactions.
