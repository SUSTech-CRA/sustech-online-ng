## 讲座简介
Web of Science（WOS）平台是理工科文献调研中的重要数据库，本讲介绍WOS平台及其SCI、ESI等子数据库；学会利用WOS平台的引文信息，发掘课题脉络；利用ESI了解科研热点和前沿；并通过检索及引文跟踪实时掌握最新动态。Web of Science (WOS) is an important database for literature research. This workshop will introduce the WOS platform and its databases such as SCI and ESI; You will learn the retrieval skills by utilizing citation and topic network ; learn to use ESI for research hotspots and frontiers, and the latest development through searching and citation tracking.
