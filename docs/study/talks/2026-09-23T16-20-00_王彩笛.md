## 讲座简介
助力学术探索，掌握外文文献检索技能是基础！本讲聚焦图书馆常用外文数据库，通过10个案例带你掌握主题检索、期刊检索、会议检索、机构检索、作者检索五类检索的思路与技巧。

English literature retrieval is the fundamental skill of academic research. This workshop introduces the commonly used English databases of SUSTech library and uses 10 cases to help you grasp 5 types of search strategies and skills, including topic search, journal search, conference search, institutional search, and author search.
