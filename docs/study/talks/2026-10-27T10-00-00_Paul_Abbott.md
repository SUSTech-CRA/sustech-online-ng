## 主讲人简介
Paul Abbott is an Adjunct Professor in the School of Physics, Mathematics, and Computing at the University of Western Australia, where he earned his physics PhD in 1987. He worked at Wolfram Research Inc (makers of Mathematica) from 1989 to 1992 and is currently a WRI consultant. Founding technical editor of The Mathematica Journal (columnist to 2010), he co-authored MathLink (2001), helped win the SIAM 100-Dollar, 100-Digit Challenge, and received an Australian University Teaching Award in 2016.
